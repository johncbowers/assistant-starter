# Skill catalog

Starting points, by the shape of the work. Most jobs have two or three of
these. Adapt the names, columns and lead times to what the user tells you;
none of this is a default to impose.

## Shapes

| Shape | One instance is… | Steps count from… | Example skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Event series** | one event (talk, workshop, webinar, meetup) | the event date, backwards | seminar, speaker series, training sessions, community events |
| **Pipeline** | one person or deal moving through stages | the last stage change, forwards | hiring, sales leads, admissions, vendor onboarding, job applications |
| **Project / client tracker** | one project or client engagement | milestones and a check-in rhythm | client projects, consulting engagements, case files (labels only) |
| **Deadline cycle** | one submission or report | a fixed due date, backwards | grant proposals, quarterly reports, budget requests, tax filings, conference papers |
| **People rhythm** | one person the user meets with regularly | the last meeting, forwards | 1:1s with direct reports, mentoring, advisor meetings |
| **Requests queue** | one thing someone asked the user to do | when it was asked | reviews and referee reports, recommendation letters, favors, approvals |

## By kind of job

**Early-career anything**
- `job-search` (pipeline): applications, stage, last contact, follow up 7
  days after any silence, thank-you note within 24 hours of an interview.
- `learning` (deadline cycle): certifications, courses, renewal dates.

**Academic (faculty, postdoc, grad student)**
- `seminar` (event series): see `examples/event-series/`.
- `reviews-letters` (requests queue): referee reports and recommendation
  letters with due dates; letters for students use labels.
- `grants` (deadline cycle): internal deadline (often 1–2 weeks before the
  sponsor's), budget to the grants office, letters of support.
- `advising` (people rhythm): meetings with advisees, by label.

**Manager or team lead**
- `one-on-ones` (people rhythm): each report, cadence, work topics to raise
  next time, last meeting. Performance, HR, health and leave never go in
  the files, not even under a label: a per-person notes file drifts that
  way, so say so in the skill. If that's the main thing they'd track,
  label the people (rule 4) and keep it to dates and work topics.
- `hiring` (pipeline): candidates by label, stage, interviewers, next step.
- `reports` (deadline cycle): weekly/monthly/quarterly reports and who gets them.

**Client-facing (consulting, agency, legal, sales, freelance)**
- `clients` (project tracker): each engagement, contacts, next deliverable,
  invoice dates, check-in every N days. Labels if under NDA.
- `leads` (pipeline): source, stage, last touch, follow-up rule.
- `invoices` (deadline cycle): sent, due, paid; nudge after 30 days.

**Operations, events, program coordination**
- `events` (event series): venue, catering, registration, reminders.
- `vendors` (pipeline): quotes, contracts, renewal dates.
- `onboarding` (project tracker): each new hire's checklist, by label if needed.

**Healthcare, education, social services**
- Anything about patients, students or clients is confidential: track the
  *work* (charts to finish, forms due) by label or count, never the person.
- `credentials` (deadline cycle): license renewals, CE hours, trainings.

**Content, communications, marketing**
- `content-calendar` (event series): each piece, publish date, draft due,
  review due, channels.
- `campaigns` (project tracker): goals, assets, launch, results check.

**Personal (if the user tracks life here too)**
- `home` (deadline cycle): seasonal chores, renewals, bills that aren't automatic.
- `trips` (event series): book, pack, tell work, out-of-office message.
- `gifts-birthdays` (event series): date, idea, buy-by date.
